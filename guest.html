Before You Leave

Check-out Guide

🕚 Check-out by 11:00 AM

Please ensure you have vacated the property by 11 AM. Late check-out fees apply — see Terms & Conditions. If you need a later checkout please contact us at least 48 hours in advance.

Please work through this checklist before you leave:

  1. Wash all dishes or ensure the dishwasher has been run and emptied.
  2. Wipe down kitchen surfaces, hob and oven if used.
  3. Empty the kitchen bin into the outdoor bins.
  4. Remove all food from the fridge and cupboards.
  5. Strip the beds and leave used linen in a pile on the bedroom floor.
  6. Leave used towels in the bathroom.
  7. Ensure the wood stove is fully extinguished and the guard is in place.
  8. Turn off all lights, heating and appliances.
  9. Close and lock all windows.
  10. Check all rooms for personal belongings.
  11. Lock the front door and return the key to the key safe.
  12. Send a quick message to let us know you've checked out — we appreciate it!

📦 Forgotten something?

If you've left anything behind, contact us at laraghlakeview@gmail.com as soon as possible. We'll do our best to post items back to you.
