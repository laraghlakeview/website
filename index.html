The Property

A place to breathe,
unwind & reconnect

Located along the tranquil waters of Lough Sheelin, Laragh is a charming holiday home that offers a perfect blend of comfort, space, and breathtaking scenery. With uninterrupted lake views, it's an ideal retreat for those seeking peace and relaxation.


The property features three bedrooms and two bathrooms, making it perfect for families or small groups. At the heart of the home is a bright and spacious kitchen and dining area — an ideal place to gather, cook, and share memorable meals while soaking in the stunning surroundings.


Whether you're enjoying a morning coffee overlooking the shimmering lake, unwinding after a day of exploring, or simply embracing the calm of the countryside — Laragh provides a warm and welcoming base for your stay.

Nearby Highlight

Just a 15-minute stroll away

Crover House
Hotel

A short 15-minute walk (or 5-minute drive) along the lakeshore brings you to the magnificent Crover House Hotel — a beautiful country house hotel set on a private peninsula on Lough Sheelin.


Renowned for its fine dining, warm hospitality, and stunning lakeshore setting, Crover House is a wonderful choice for a special dinner out or a relaxed afternoon drink. It's also a celebrated wedding venue — if you're attending or planning a wedding at Crover House, Laragh makes the perfect base for your guests, with the convenience of walking distance and the comfort of a private home.

Lough Sheelin

Laragh sits on the shores of Lough Sheelin in the heart of Co. Cavan — one of Ireland's most beautiful and underrated lake regions. Surrounded by rolling countryside and ancient woodland, it's a world away yet within easy reach of several charming towns.

  • 🏨 Crover House Hotel 15 min walk · 5 min drive
  • 🏘 Ballyjamesduff 10 min drive
  • 🏘 Kilnaleck 10 min drive
  • 🏘 Virginia 20 min drive
  • 🏘 Oldcastle 20 min drive
  • 🏙 Cavan Town 35 min drive
  • 🏙 Dublin ~1 hr 30 min drive
