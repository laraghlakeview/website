The Property

A home beside

Located along the tranquil waters of Lough Sheelin, this charming holiday home offers a perfect blend of comfort, space, and breathtaking scenery. With uninterrupted lake views, it's an ideal retreat for those seeking peace and relaxation.

Whether you're enjoying a morning coffee overlooking the shimmering lake, unwinding after a day of exploring, or simply embracing the calm of the countryside, Laragh Lake View provides a warm and welcoming base for your stay.
